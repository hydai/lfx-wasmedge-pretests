Pre-test

Verify the issue WasmEdge#2733 (https://github.com/WasmEdge/WasmEdge/issues/2733) for its reproducibility:

  1. Build WasmEdge from the latest master branch from source.
  2. Reproduce the issue (if still reproducible) with your build in both interpreter mode and AOT mode.
  3. Compare the behavior with other WASM runtimes (such as wasmtime).
  4. Provide the output you expect WasmEdge to produce, and explain your reasoning (refer to the WebAssembly specification where applicable).
  5. Attach the exact commands you used, the screenshots or logs of the execution results from each runtime.
